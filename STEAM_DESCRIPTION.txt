[h2]The crusader states, won back[/h2]
The Kingdom of Jerusalem, the County of Tripoli and the Principality of Antioch do [b]not[/b] exist in 1337. The map starts exactly as in vanilla. These crowns return only if Catholic Christendom wins them back.

This is a variant of [b]Crusader States — Restoration[/b], where the three states already exist at the start. [b]Use one or the other, never both.[/b]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Stonefletcher-Productions-LLC/eu5-crusader-states-restorable/main/art/workshop/three_crowns.jpg[/img]

[h2]Win a crusade[/h2]
When a crusade ends in victory, every Catholic country on the winning side becomes a crusade veteran for 50 years. Any of them that holds the seat of Jerusalem, Tripoli or Antioch gets the event [i]The Crowns of Outremer[/i]:
[list]
[*][b]Restore them under the Holy See.[/b] Each state whose seat you hold rises from the lands you hold there as a Catholic monarchy and a Crusader State of the Papal States. You gain prestige and religious influence.
[*][b]Restore them as your vassals.[/b] The same, but they serve you.
[*][b]Keep the land.[/b] You can still form them yourself later.
[/list]

[h2]Or form them[/h2]
Crusade veterans can form each state: own Jerusalem, Tripoli or Antioch and 75% of its land (Filastin; Lebanon; Antakya and Latakia). Catholic military orders can form Tripoli and Antioch too, and vanilla's own Jerusalem formable still works.

[h2]What the restored states get[/h2]
[list]
[*]The Crusader State bond with Rome: no tribute, +25 loyalty, stronger and cheaper forts.
[*]Crusader Knights heavy cavalry.
[*]Unique advances for each state, plus vanilla Jerusalem's own.
[*]Crusader volunteers for a Papal Jerusalem attacked by a larger enemy.
[*]Their own flags and map colours.
[/list]
