# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-30)

First release, for EU5 1.4.6.

- Jerusalem, Tripoli and Antioch no longer exist at the start.
- Winning a crusade lets Catholic victors restore them as Papal Crusader States or their own vassals.
- Crusade veterans (and, for Tripoli and Antioch, military orders) can form them.
- Crusader State subject type, Crusader Knights, advances, volunteers, flags and localization carried over from Crusader States — Restoration 0.6.0.
